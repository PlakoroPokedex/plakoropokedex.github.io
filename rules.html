Plakoro Battle Rulebook

1. Setup 2. Turn Structure 3. Double Battle 4. Two-on-Two Team Battle 5. Three- or Four-Player Battle Royale 6. Official Bandai Format 7. U.S. Legacy Format 8. League Format

1. Setup

  1. Each player prepares 1 Pokémon die (Charakoro) and 3 Energy dice (Enekoro).
  2. Each player prepares 1 Pokémon card, 4 move cards their Pokémon can use, 1 Life Counter, and 1 play sheet.
  3. Place your Pokémon card face up. Show your opponent your Charakoro and your 3 regular Enekoro.
  4. Choose 4 move cards and place them face down. When both players are ready, reveal the cards together.
  5. Set each Pokémon’s Life Counter to 120 HP.
  6. Play rock-paper-scissors. The winner goes first.

2. Turn Structure

  1. Choose a move. Say the move’s name and place its card in the move space. Choose a different move from the one you used on your last turn.
  2. Roll your dice. Roll your Charakoro and 3 Enekoro together. On the first turn of a Battle, the player going first rolls only 2 Enekoro. The second player starts with 3.
  3. Check the energy cost. Match the energy symbols you rolled to the symbols on the move card. Count both symbols on a double-energy face. Any energy symbol can pay a colorless cost.
  4. If you are missing energy, the move fails. Skip the move’s effects, including its Charakoro effect. Your turn ends.
  5. If you have enough energy, the move succeeds. Follow the move card’s instructions. If your Charakoro lands in a position shown on the card, apply the effect beside that picture. If no picture matches, use the move’s regular effect.
  6. Update HP. Apply the move’s damage and effects. If a damaging move’s type matches the target’s weakness, add 20 damage. A Pokémon is knocked out when its HP reaches 0.
  7. Pass the turn. In a Single Battle, you win as soon as the opposing Pokémon is knocked out.

Example turn. Your move costs 1 Darkness energy and 2 colorless energy. You roll 1 Darkness and 2 Water. The move succeeds because the Water symbols can pay the colorless cost. If you roll 3 Water instead, the move fails because you are missing the Darkness symbol.

3. Double Battle

  1. Each player prepares 2 Pokémon. Bring 1 Charakoro and 4 move cards for each Pokémon, plus 6 regular Enekoro to share between them.
  2. Both Pokémon start in battle. Choose 1 Pokémon to attack on your turn.
  3. Choose either opposing Pokémon as your target. Roll your attacker’s Charakoro and any 3 of your 6 Enekoro.
  4. Alternate attackers while both of your Pokémon are able to attack. If only 1 can attack, use that Pokémon.
  5. Knock out both opposing Pokémon to win.

4. Two-on-Two Team Battle

  1. Form 2 teams of 2 players. Each player prepares 1 Pokémon, 1 Charakoro, 3 regular Enekoro, and 4 move cards.
  2. Teammates must choose different Pokémon.
  3. Team A’s first player goes first, followed by Team B’s first player, Team A’s second player, and Team B’s second player. Repeat that order.
  4. On your turn, choose either opposing Pokémon as your target. Roll your Charakoro and 3 Enekoro.
  5. Knock out both Pokémon on the opposing team to win.

5. Three- or Four-Player Battle Royale

  1. Each player prepares 1 Pokémon, 1 Charakoro, 3 regular Enekoro, and 4 move cards. Everyone plays for themselves.
  2. Each player rolls 2 Enekoro on their own first turn. On later turns, roll 3 Enekoro.
  3. When choosing a target, check who was targeted by the previous attack. You must choose a different opponent if another target is available.
  4. When only 2 players remain, they may attack each other every turn.
  5. The last player whose Pokémon still has HP wins.

6. Official Bandai Format

  1. Each player chooses 1 Pokémon for the event.
  2. Play 3 rounds, with 1 game per round.
  3. Use the same Pokémon for all 3 rounds.
  4. You may change your 4 move cards between rounds.

7. U.S. Legacy Format

Use the 2026 battle rules with the following changes.

Pokémon and move cards

  1. You may use Pokémon cards, move cards, Charakoro, and Enekoro from 1997 and 2026.
  2. You may pair a Pokémon’s 1997 Charakoro with its 2026 card, or its 2026 Charakoro with its 1997 card.
  3. A Pokémon may use its moves from either year. Normal, Strong, and Legendary versions of the same Pokémon may use each other’s moves. For example, Pikachu may use Strong Pikachu’s moves, and Strong Pikachu may use Pikachu’s moves.
  4. Surfing Pikachu may use any Pikachu’s moves. Only Surfing Pikachu may use Surfing Pikachu’s own moves.
  5. Each Pokémon may bring up to 1 Special Move. That Special Move may be used once per game.
  6. 1997 Pokémon have no weakness. Each 1997 move has the same type as the Pokémon it belongs to.
  7. You may prepare proxy move cards if you have the correct pokemon to use them but don't own the cards.

Energy dice

  1. Use only the official custom chips made for that generation of Enekoro.
  2. 1997 Enekoro have 4 single-energy faces that stay fixed once the die is assembled. You may change the 2 double-energy faces.
  3. 2026 Enekoro have 2 fixed painted faces. You may change the other 4 faces using 2 single-energy chips and 2 double-energy chips.
  4. If a move costs Normal energy, any energy symbol can pay that cost.
  5. If an effect tells you to roll Normal energy, you must roll an actual Normal symbol, found only on 1997 Enekoro.

8. League Format

Prepare your party

  1. League Singles: Each player prepares 6 Pokémon and 9 Enekoro. You will choose 3 Pokémon for the match and use 1 at a time.
  2. League Doubles: Each player prepares 6 Pokémon and 12 Enekoro. You will choose 4 Pokémon for the match and use 2 at a time.
  3. Show your opponent all 6 Pokémon in your party. After both parties are revealed, secretly choose the Pokémon you will use in this match.
  4. Choose 4 move cards for each selected Pokémon.
  5. Send out your starting Pokémon. The other Pokémon you selected stay in your party until you send them out. Pokémon you left out of your selection cannot enter the match.

Use SWITCH

  1. On your turn, you may use SWITCH. Every player has this action in addition to their Pokémon’s 4 move cards.
  2. Return 1 active Pokémon to your party. Send out 1 other party Pokémon that still has HP and is waiting to enter battle.
  3. Roll the incoming Pokémon’s Characoro. If it lands Standing, that Pokémon may attack immediately. On any other result, the incoming Pokémon stays in battle and your turn ends.

Knockouts and winning

  1. When an active Pokémon reaches 0 HP, send out a party Pokémon that still has HP and is waiting to enter battle, if you have one.
  2. In League Singles, knock out all 3 of your opponent’s selected Pokémon to win.
  3. In League Doubles, knock out all 4 of your opponent’s selected Pokémon to win.
